Symposium AI 2.3 lets you talk to your AIs, and makes sure long answers never just stop.

SPEAK YOUR PROMPT
Tap the new microphone in the message box to dictate instead of typing. It works in Chat, Compare, and Create, and in the custom motion box when you set up a debate. Your words appear as you speak, with punctuation added for you. Tap again to stop, then edit before you send. Dictation uses your device's built-in speech recognition and works without an API key.

FINISH WHAT THEY STARTED
When a reply hits the length limit, it is now clearly marked as cut off instead of looking complete. Tap Continue and the same AI picks up exactly where it stopped, in the same message.
