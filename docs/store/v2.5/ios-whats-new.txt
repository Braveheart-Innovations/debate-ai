Symposium AI 2.5 brings Moonshot's newest Kimi model and moves you off models their providers have shut down.

NEW MODELS
Kimi K3.1 Pro is the new Kimi default: Moonshot's newest flagship, a third cheaper than Kimi K3, with a 1M-token context. Kimi K3.1 Pro High-Speed is the same model at two to three times the speed.

RETIRED MODELS
• Moonshot is retiring the Kimi K2 models. Chats and debates that used Kimi K2.6 or K2.7 Code now use Kimi K3.1 Pro.
• Google shut down Imagen 4. Imagen 4, Imagen 4 Fast and Imagen 4 Ultra are gone from Create, and a saved choice of any of them now creates with Gemini 3.1 Flash Image instead of failing.

FIXES
Replies are no longer cut short. The app sets no response length unless you choose one, so Cohere, Perplexity and models that think before they answer can finish, in chats and in voice debates. Changing another setting, like temperature, no longer adds a hidden limit either.

UNDER THE HOOD
Updated app framework and navigation libraries, with security fixes.

And as always, bring your own API keys. They stay private and secure on your device.
